How to Use This Model

1. Enter Your Initial Position

Set your starting cash, target leverage, and entry price for silver. This determines your initial position size.

2. Understand the Problem

When silver rises, your unrealized gains increase your account equity. But since your position size stays the same, your leverage ratio drops.

Example: With 5x leverage at $60, you have 8,333 oz. At $100, you've made $333k, so your equity is now $433k. Your leverage is now only $833k / $433k = 1.92x.

3. Use the Rebalancing Table

The table shows, at each price level:

  • Current Leverage: Your actual leverage ratio with current position
  • Additional Oz Needed: How many oz to buy at this price to get back to 5x
  • Cost to Buy: How much capital required for that purchase
  • Total Position: Your new position size after rebalancing

4. Key Formula

To maintain 5x leverage as your equity grows:

  • Target Notional = Account Equity × Leverage Multiple
  • Current Notional = Current Position Size × Current Price
  • Additional Oz = (Target Notional − Current Notional) / Current Price

5. Trading Strategy

As silver rises:

  1. Check the rebalancing table at the current price
  2. See how many additional oz you need
  3. Calculate the cost (shown in "Cost to Buy")
  4. If you have capital available, buy that amount to rebalance
  5. Your position size increases, maintaining your 5x leverage

This pyramids your position up as silver rises, capturing more upside while maintaining constant leverage discipline.

6. Capital Requirements

Note that each rebalancing requires additional capital (margin or fresh cash). The model assumes you have access to enough margin to support these additional purchases at your target leverage.
