How to Use This Model

1. Enter Your Parameters

Update the three input fields on the left. The model recalculates in real-time.

2. Understand Your Position

Position Size: Number of ounces you control with your capital and leverage.

Notional Exposure: Total market value you're exposed to (Cash × Leverage).

Max Loss: Dollar amount you can lose before hitting your leverage limit (Equity ÷ Leverage).

Initial Stop: Where your first stop should be placed.

3. Use the Price Scenarios Table

The table shows what happens at different price levels. The Required Stop column tells you where to place your stop at each price.

For example: If silver reaches $100, your stop should be around the $100 row's "Required Stop" price.

As silver rises and you make gains, your equity grows, so your dollar risk budget increases—your stop moves higher.

4. Key Concept: Maintaining Leverage

This model maintains constant leverage ratio by adjusting your stop as your equity grows:

  • Max Loss = Account Equity ÷ Leverage Multiple
  • Stop Price = Current Price − (Max Loss ÷ Position Size)
  • As price rises → equity grows → max loss grows → stop moves higher

5. Risk Management

If silver hits your required stop price, you'll lose exactly (Account Equity ÷ Leverage), maintaining your leverage discipline.

Higher leverage = bigger position = more profit potential AND greater risk. Use accordingly.
